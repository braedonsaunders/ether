# Brand marks

Local copies only. The app does not fetch logos at runtime.

Each SVG is the path geometry from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), which publishes these marks under [CC0-1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.14.0/LICENSE.md). The app, integration, and catalogue marks were retrieved 2026-10-30 from v11.14.0 (`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/11.14.0/icons/<slug>.svg`). Debrid-Link and Stremio were not in that release; they were retrieved 2026-10-31 from v16.36.0 under the same CC0 terms.

Sanitized on retrieval: XML declaration, titles, roles, and every element other than one `viewBox="0 0 24 24"` root and its `path` data were removed. Fill is `currentColor`. No scripts, external references, or event handlers.

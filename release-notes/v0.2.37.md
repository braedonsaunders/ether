Create an opt-in “Look Ahead” collection from the whole catalogue using **Release dates → Upcoming** and a 1–366 day window. **Both** combines released and upcoming matches. Existing collections and normal catalogue browsing/search remain released-only by default. Upcoming placeholders show their scheduled release date, require a collection visible to the viewer, and reject playback before searching sources.

This first version supports movie release dates and new-series first-air dates in the Collections tab. It does not include future episodes/seasons, automatic Home rows, poster grayscale, or early-release searching. Future placeholders use base TMDB artwork; overlays and custom poster providers remain for released titles. TMDB discovery is bounded and warns about truncated results; dates refresh on the collection’s schedule and may change. Authoritative date refresh is limited to 500 titles or 15 seconds per evaluation; unverified upcoming entries are excluded with a warning, while already-released mixed members remain.

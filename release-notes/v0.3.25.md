# Ether v0.3.25

Highlights:

- **Playback can adjust to your connection.** Vela can switch from a buffering original to adaptive streaming at the same position, then raise quality again as the connection improves. Jellyfin and Emby can also request adaptive streams.
- **Fewer calls to debrid services.** Ether paces requests per account, remembers answers and shares work across episodes in the same pack.
- **New releases with less indexer traffic.** Ether watches feeds for matching releases, reuses searches and respects each indexer's daily allowance.
- **Catch-up remembers where you stopped.** Resume an airing or mark a whole catch-up season watched (#872, #876, #880, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Less waiting for large libraries and playlists.** Xtream playlist refreshes reuse a saved lineup, and Silo, Vela and the console do less work on common screens.
- **See which recommendations get watched.** Activity › Recommendations shows what viewers start from each row, and the console's Best rows panel shows the observed play rate.

## Playback and apps

- **Jellyfin on Roku and Shield.** Roku gets chapter markers when it opens the item, and Ether checks the device's limits when planning playback for files with Dolby Vision or TrueHD. When Roku has no compatible playback path, Jellyfin gets `NoCompatibleStream`. Embedded subtitle extraction has a three-second deadline, and series pages follow Jellyfin's response schema (#759 and vela#42, thanks @krakenn9).
- **Plex for Roku keeps 4K when it can.** Playback keeps the media decision already made for the session when Roku leaves out `hasMDE` at start. A 4K video can keep its original picture when only the audio needs converting (#892, thanks @jmiller0).
- **Vela downloads** can start right after sign-in or a server restart because the app can send its device capabilities with the request. A download grant includes audio and subtitle tracks, chapters, and intro and credits markers when known. Cached text subtitles can come with the same ticket, and refusals explain what held up the download.
- **Jellyfin and Emby downloads** report the exact file size when known and can send back watched state and resume points after offline viewing. If a Vela, Jellyfin or Emby download is interrupted, the transfer fails explicitly so a short byte-range response can't pass as a complete chunk.
- **YouTube trailers in Vela** can play through YouTube's embed on the viewer's device. Owners can turn this on in Apps › Vela › Trailers, including on servers without a trailer resolver (vela#81, thanks @Motley-Brew).
- **Local files first** is an option in Releases › Profiles. Ether can play a suitable file from a granted folder before fetching another copy (#873, thanks @denMaier).
- **Multi-disc releases** pick the right disc and audio track: Ether matches audio tracks across discs and chooses the right archive in nested or per-disc sets (#860, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Catch-up plays in Plex for iOS.** The play queue now names the selected airing, so iOS no longer opens an empty player (#871, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Subtitles from the first play with add-on sources.** With a Stremio add-on source such as AIOStreams, Plex apps now get the file's audio and subtitle tracks, and your default subtitle choice, on the very first play instead of after changing a track. Subtitles an add-on supplies are fetched when needed, and Vela lists them as external tracks.
- **Auto keeps your language rules.** With original-language audio or the viewer's subtitle language turned on, Auto no longer picks a dubbed or subtitle-less release just because a device (for example Plex on NVIDIA Shield) can play it directly. Ether converts the audio or renders the subtitles instead. A release whose audio Ether has read and found dubbed is passed over even when its name doesn't say so, and a warm, cached copy that meets the rules is preferred.
- **Chinese subtitles** from SubSource are found again, using the service's search codes for Simplified and Traditional Chinese (#875, thanks @beauClash).

## Live TV

- **Catch-up watch state** follows the viewer in Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Silo and Vela. Airings keep their resume points and watched marks while they're in the archive. Marking a show or season watched or unwatched applies to the airings that viewer can play (#872, #876, #880, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Now Playing shows the Live TV provider** serving a channel, including the Xtream login when applicable (#864, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Tuning can wait briefly for a provider slot** when all connections are in use. Owners can set how long Ether waits without going over the provider's connection limit (#868, thanks @drondeseries).

## Home and rows

- **Collection cards use the saved look.** Collections on Home can show square logos, landscape art or posters in Vela, and the console preview uses the chosen shape too (#881, thanks @Motley-Brew).
- **Not interested in Vela** removes a title from your Home and library rows and records the preference for future recommendations. You can undo it or give a title a thumbs-up (vela#82, thanks @Motley-Brew).

## Transcoding

- **Adaptive HLS** is available for compatible playback in Vela, Jellyfin and Emby. Ether can include the original picture as the highest quality when its segments line up, and starts encoding other qualities as the player asks for them.
- **Vela can recover from sustained buffering on direct play.** When Ether sees delivery falling behind, it can switch an adaptive session at the current position to qualities that fit the measured connection. The player can climb back up within the offered qualities as delivery improves. Viewer and server limits still apply.

## Posters and artwork

- **Plex apps use Plex's metadata by default** when the server is claimed. The setting is under Apps › Plex › Account.
- **Playlist artwork** can load without reading the entire playlist, and cover lookups on the same page share work.

## Console

- **Activity › Recommendations** shows impressions, plays, completion and watch time by row, with viewer and date filters. **Best rows** on the console overview shows the observed play rate and labels rows with too little data to rank.
- **Sources › Indexers** shows daily usage and what spent it, including plays, background preparation and title detail views. Each indexer can have a daily request cap, and Ether pauses an indexer when Prowlarr reports that its limit has been reached. Searches go to sources that handle the relevant categories and reuse recent results when possible.
- **New releases** settings for TMDB movies and shows are available through Libraries › Titles come from › Manage. In **When they can play** mode, Ether makes an initial availability lookup and then watches release feeds for matches, much like Radarr and Sonarr monitor new releases.
- **Releases › History includes music.** Open a play or a failed attempt to see the chosen release or why candidates were turned down. A search cut short by a deadline or a temporary source problem can be retried.
- **Easynews speed tests** verify that the provider has the sample before timing it. If Ether can't find a usable sample, it explains that in place of a misleading slow speed.
- **Pins can fall back.** When you pin a release in Releases › Pinned, or pin a whole season, choose **Strict** (as before: if the release can't play, Auto playback stops) or **Prefer, with fallback**: Ether plays the pinned release when it can and otherwise picks the next best release, then tries the pin again later. The Pinned list shows each pin's mode and the last reason a preference could not be used (#893).
- **Viewers has tabs:** People, Watchlists (on Home, sources and alerts) and Security (sign-in lockouts), instead of everything stacked under the viewer list.
- **Setup can skip Sources or Indexers.** Choose **Skip this step** and finish the rest of the wizard, then configure either page later.
- **Notifications opens again** when a channel has no events selected (#877, thanks @jmiller0).

## Viewer portal

- **Download quality is clearer.** The viewer's **Highest download quality** setting explains that it applies to downloads and links to the quality rules for setting a playback limit. Ether also applies playback limits when reusing sessions (#854, thanks @steelricky).
- **Revoking a Vela device grant** also revokes the refresh credentials issued with it, so that device has to sign in again.

## Plugins

- **Plugin libraries look right in the console.** A plugin's own library icon appears where available, and libraries exclusive to a plugin's app hide the Tabs settings for other apps. Video release rules are hidden for both adapter-only and non-video libraries (#884, #885, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Larger saves and save states** can upload without the plugin hashing the file itself. Owners can raise the upload ceiling and set each plugin's durable storage quota in its Limits. Downloads of those files stream directly from Ether's storage (#870, #882, #891, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Cache › Libraries** lets owners set space and file-size limits for plugin library downloads. Those files and playback segments share the media cache budget, and Cache Explorer lists both (#894, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Local folders** can match folders using TMDB, TVDB or IMDb tags, and differences in title punctuation no longer need a manual match (#874, thanks @beauClash).
- **Plugin errors are clearer.** When a plugin call fails, library cards, the plugin's Overview and Activity, and sync jobs say why in a plain sentence (for example a rejected API key, rate limiting, or a timeout), with the raw route and status under Details. Release history gives the reason a plugin candidate was rejected, and a download that runs out of preparation time tells the app when to retry (#887, thanks @FizzWhirl).

## Plugins (developers)

- **Uploads and durable storage.** Staged uploads include SHA-256, SHA-1, MD5 and CRC-32. Routes can declare up to 512 MiB per upload, subject to the owner's limit. Adapters can reply with their own durable blob for Ether to stream, and `cache.usage({durable: true})` reports usage and quotas (#870, #882, #891, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Granted folders.** `folders.roots()` lists the folders the owner gave that plugin, and `plugin.folders.changed` tells it when to check again (#883, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Adapter downloads can wait for preparation.** Set `wait` on a `frontend.streamURL` ticket to keep the download request open within a time limit while it joins the shared preparation job (#887, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Preferred sources and parallel ranges.** `frontend.streamURL` can try a preferred source first and fall back to other sources for the same item and part. A compatible HTTP range source can use multiple connections for read-ahead, and `http.fetchToCache` accepts ranges from replicas with matching strong validators and file lengths (#895, #896, #855, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Resumable catalogue syncs.** Plugins with replayable pages of upserts can opt into checkpoints so a sync can pick up after an interruption.
- **Debrid cache hints.** Source and integration plugins can declare `debrid_cache_hints` to tell Ether which shortlisted torrents are already cached when a viewer presses Play in Vela or Silo (#879, #897, thanks @theatarr).

## Performance

- **Xtream M3U refreshes** reuse an on-demand lineup snapshot for each viewer. Ether batches the build and refreshes existing snapshots in the background. On the baseline test library, building the lineup dropped from about 138,000 database statements on the request to about 1,600 in the background.
- **Silo browsing and playlists** do less work opening search results, cards and playlist pages because Ether checks access and loads details in batches for the page.
- **Vela starts with less database work.** Opening Home and loading playlists batch the lookups, and repeated playback capability reports avoid redundant writes. Playback updates for a changing clock are sent less often, while track and stream changes still arrive immediately.
- **Console viewer management** does less work merging or deleting accounts, especially when the account has little history. Opening a viewer's taste settings can show the first result without waiting for every Home row to build.
- **On Air, Live TV and plugin screens** skip redundant full scans and per-item lookups (#862, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Performance checks cover more everyday screens,** including Subsonic album and genre browsing, Jellyfin music favorites, Vela and Silo browsing, and console and viewer portal workflows.

## Reliability

- **Debrid accounts are paced individually.** Ether honors the service's retry delay and backs off when limits persist. Trial adds are reserved for Play, with an allowance to prepare the immediate next episode during playback. Ether remembers cached, uncached, pending, blocked and too-large answers per account, and shares acquisition work when episodes use the same pack. A refusal can apply to a whole torrent or just one file, while a temporary connection failure can be retried. Sources › Debrid shows API attempts, retries, rate limits, account reuses, remembered answers and average calls per first play.
- **AllDebrid magnets you already hold** can be reused while downloading or ready. Ether checks current account status before adding a magnet so cleanup leaves existing ones alone (#889, thanks @theatarr).
- **Torznab cache hints** let Ether try a marked debrid account first when an indexer supplies `cachedon`. Ether still checks that the account can serve the file (#879, #886, thanks @theatarr).
- **Plex watchlists and IDs:** a friend's watchlist entry that matches more than one title is left unmatched instead of picking the wrong one, and Plex's newer external-ID arrays are read without colliding with the main guid (#861, #863, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Usenet accounts on the same backbone** can share a missing-article answer. Set **Backbone** in the provider's options to avoid asking sibling accounts for the same missing article (#888, thanks @zab1996).

## Security

Thanks @theatarr for responsible disclosure (#878).

- **Live TV:** a malformed stream can no longer stop the server.
- Non-owners can no longer delete a subtitle the owner uploaded.
- Ask Ether masks secret flow parameters and only opens links you or its search provided.

## Upgrade notes

- **Vela 0.2.81** or later is recommended.
- **Plex metadata now defaults to on** for claimed servers. Earlier versions never stored "off", so if you had turned it off, turn it off again under Apps › Plex › Account.
- **First start runs database migrations,** including filling in catch-up show and season keys for stored guide entries.

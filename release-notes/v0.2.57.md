This release adds opt-in per-viewer Plex account watch-state and rating sync (#143).

## Plex Account Watch-State Sync

- **Per-viewer opt-in Plex watch-state and rating sync:** each viewer can connect their own Plex account under **Viewers → Access** and enable future sync to publish watched/unwatched marks and 1–5 star ratings to `scrobbles.plex.tv`, with manual **Import** and **Export** controls for existing history (#143).
- **Local playback remains authoritative:** sync delivery is asynchronous, bounded, and fenced per viewer so neither a Plex outage nor an unmapped GUID delays or blocks local playback, and guest accounts cannot bind the server owner's Plex token (#143).

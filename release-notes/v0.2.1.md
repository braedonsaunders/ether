Ether 0.2.1 improves desktop compatibility:

- The universal Mac app targets macOS 13 and newer on Intel and Apple Silicon.
- The Windows tray opens without an extra console window.

Ether 0.2.1 brings the current owner UI, personalized discovery, Live TV, debrid streaming, and server hardening into one release.

### Downloads

- **macOS 13+:** `Ether-0.2.1.dmg`, a universal Apple Silicon + Intel menu-bar app. Drag Ether to Applications and open it; the app starts its per-user server.
- **Windows x64:** `EtherSetup-0.2.1.exe`, with the server service and tray launcher. Standalone Windows ARM64 binaries are also available.
- **Linux amd64 / arm64:** DEB, RPM, and standalone archives.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/braedonsaunders/ether:0.2.1` (amd64 / arm64).

Open http://localhost:32400/ether/ after starting Ether. Installers and archives bundle FFmpeg and FFprobe 8.1.3.

### Included

- Reworked owner navigation and settings, layered HomeScreen design, per-viewer controls, and mixed movie/TV Home rows.
- Richer default recommendations, crowd co-engagement and trend signals, and a bounded learned ranker that trains outside playback activity.
- Multiple ordered inputs per Live TV channel, source health and backups, authenticated IPTV sources, compressed XMLTV guides, guide matching, and an owner preview player.
- Real-Debrid, TorBox, AllDebrid, and Premiumize integrations; source routing, connection budgets, startup warming, and recovery from interrupted delivery.
- Apple TV playback compatibility improvements, including HDR10 fallback for supported Dolby Vision profile 8.1 sources, plus protections against unsafe direct-play source replacement.
- Viewer/session isolation and access revocation fixes, rotating logs, service recovery, cache/database maintenance, and signed automatic-update manifests.
- Optional PostgreSQL-backed clustering with shared settings, leader election, session coordination, draining, and account-budget sharing.

### Signing and installation

The macOS app is **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after the first launch attempt. The Windows installer is **not Authenticode-signed** and may trigger SmartScreen. Ether's update manifest is separately signed with its Ed25519 release key; SHA-256 checksums are attached.

Ether ships no content, providers, site definitions, accounts, or third-party API keys. Supply your own TMDB key and service credentials. Plex and TMDB are independent third-party services; Ether is not affiliated with or endorsed by either.

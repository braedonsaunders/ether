This release improves anime source matching, preserves audio selections, adds optional upcoming-episode listings, and recognizes both Fire TV Stick 4K Max models.

## Changes

- Preserve a pre-play audio choice when the release has a different track order. Choosing estimated English no longer selects French merely because French occupies the same position after probing. Regional language matches take priority over codec hints; missing languages fall back to viewer preferences.
- Match searches such as “Taskmaster 2015” and “Dune (2021)” by retrying an empty literal search without the year and preferring the requested year. Real numeric titles retain literal matches.
- Rank accented exact title matches, such as “Shōgun,” ahead of partial unaccented matches.
- Honor exact movie/show GUID filters on Plex library lookups. Unknown, malformed or disallowed identities no longer return an unrelated library page.
- Accept correctly numbered anime BluRay releases tagged `BD` or `BDMux`, recognize additional three-letter audio languages, and compare familiar contractions such as `I.ll` and `I'll` consistently. Wrong episode titles, part numbers and episode numbers remain rejected. Parsed search caches are refreshed once after this update.

- Add an opt-in Catalog setting, **Show scheduled episodes**, for dated, unaired episodes in seasons that have already started. Entries show an Unaired label and scheduled air date, expose no playable media, and retain viewer restrictions. Unknown air dates, wholly future seasons and alternate episode-order lists are unchanged.
- Recognize both exact Fire TV Stick 4K Max model identifiers (AFTKA and AFTKRT) when Plex reports them as Android. These models can select 2160p/HDR10; explicit client quality and bitrate limits still apply. This does not force Dolby Vision or override other Android devices.

After updating, reopen any item that was already open before choosing its audio track, so Plex refreshes the stream list.

These failures were reproduced in offline regressions. No live Apple TV or provider/indexer account was used. The reported Plex Watchlist display still needs a native-client retest; the verified repair is the GUID lookup API. Search accent folding applies to result ranking, not the offline database matching step.

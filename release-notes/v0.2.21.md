Improves subtitle discovery in Plex, includes Intel VA-API drivers in Docker, and refreshes owner subscription features while Ether is running.

## Major changes

- **Subtitles in Plex:** expose cached OpenSubtitles tracks to clients and add subtitle search.
- **Docker VA-API support:** include the DRM loader and Intel drivers needed for supported hardware-transcoding setups. Host device access and compatible hardware are still required.
- **Subscription refresh:** recheck the owner's Plex Pass while Ether runs, so subscription changes do not require a restart to be noticed.
- Remove blank rows from the setup wizard.

**Full changes:** [v0.2.21 commit comparison](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/compare/v0.2.20...v0.2.21).

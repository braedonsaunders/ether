A focused maintenance update: the updater now distinguishes an unchecked or failed update feed from a confirmed up-to-date installation.

## Changes

Maintenance no longer says “Ether is up to date” before the first update check or after an update-feed check fails. It now shows “Not checked yet” or the reported error. Known available releases remain visible and installable, and disabled-update and rollback explanations are preserved.

This corrects the status display; it does not change update installation or establish a fix for certificate trust or addon playback reports.

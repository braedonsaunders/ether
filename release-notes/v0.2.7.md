Ether 0.2.7 improves Plex playback reliability, catalogue warming, and personalised recommendations.

- Fix the main Play button on Plex Web show pages by returning one On Deck episode.
- Keep active play queues alive during long sessions and make queue eviction deterministic.
- Keep DASH rendition changes from inheriting an earlier seek's end-of-stream boundary and rejecting the final segment.
- Retry Windows sharing violations without silently losing completed media segments or final playlists.
- Protect casting with short-lived, server-local grants rather than exposing account tokens.
- Contain and reap encoder process trees across Unix and Windows, with safer replacement and shutdown ordering.
- Reject compressed RAR sources earlier and reject implausible yEnc ranges.
- Keep language ranking neutral by default and expand recorded Plex protocol coverage.
- Update Vitest test tooling to address two dependency security advisories.
- Improve catalogue warming and freshness, expand recommendation signals and variety, and improve same-day episode resolution.

Includes the native macOS desktop app and DMG, Windows desktop installer, Linux packages, multi-architecture container images, and signed automatic-update manifests.

Validation includes the release commit's Mac, Windows, and Linux CI, plus the earlier cross-platform resilience campaign covering simulated long-lived queues, protocol load, concurrent transcoding, and decoded-output verification. These checks do not guarantee uninterrupted playback under every source or network failure.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized; macOS may require Open Anyway. The Windows installer is unsigned and may trigger SmartScreen. Update manifests are cryptographically signed separately.

Ether ships no content, providers, or indexers. Owners supply their own services and are responsible for what they access.

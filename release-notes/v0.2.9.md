Ether 0.2.9 improves playback continuity, startup, discovery, and background reliability.

- Stream source discovery as results arrive and prepare next-episode playback ahead of time.
- Preserve paused sessions, buffered media, picture size, and audio levels during source recovery.
- Fix post-play Up Next playback and report the actual client playhead in Activity.
- Put Continue Watching first and prioritize personal picks, new releases, and trending titles.
- Use bounded indexer feeds to establish availability for recent movies and episodes without catalogue-wide searches; retain historical-title visibility.
- Recover feed catch-up across shifting results and restarts, while preserving quotas and fresh-release polling.
- Recover scheduled background work after sleep, downtime, and clock corrections.

Desktop installers for Windows and macOS, Linux packages, server archives, and signed update manifests are attached. Ether ships no content or provider accounts.

## Discovery, collections and library visibility

- Hourly global TMDB movie/TV trending with durable daily and weekly charts; Continue Watching first and Trending Now second in default layouts.
- Stable browsing visits with renewed category variety on a new client session or after inactivity.
- Separate library collections from Home/Recommended rows; default movie genres and TV networks/studios with local dynamic matching.
- New Library Visibility settings with combined country, language, genre, year and rating rules, and a local preview. The existing Explore your catalog page is retained. No exclusions are enabled automatically.
- Updated Collections placement editor and trending freshness panel.

Validated with pure-Go build, vet and full tests, 241 UI tests, and desktop/narrow-layout inspection. Country rules use available TMDB origin/production metadata; unknown values remain visible.

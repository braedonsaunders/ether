# Ether v0.3.10

Highlights:

- **Live TV is a setup choice, and an AI builds channels with you.** Setup asks whether you want Live TV. Turning it on adds the four decade channels, and with it off Live TV costs nothing. A new channel builder interviews you (what the channel is for, commercials or none, branding) and programs it, YouTube commercials included. You approve every change before it airs.
- **Virtual channels keep the wall clock.** A channel never schedules a title without a playable source, and a replacement title joins mid-slot at the right point instead of starting from the beginning. On Air graphics never hold programming back. A tune opens on the channel's complete title card from the very first frame (never black or an off-air card), the animated version takes over as soon as it is ready, and the programme follows four seconds later at the right point. Rendering is capped at one low-priority job, only for what the channel needs next.
- **Step aside works on every Plex surface at library scale** ([#418](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/discussions/418)). Titles you already have on your own Plex server are hidden from home rows, section pages, search and the letter jump bar, including multi-episode files such as `S04E22-E23`.
- **Each version brings its own subtitles** ([#461](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/461)). Every release offered under Play Version, in Plex and in Jellyfin/Swiftfin, lists its own embedded subtitle tracks, and picking a version plays that release's tracks. PGS subtitles on Apple TV are carried through remuxes.
- **Pause a source without deleting it.** Debrid accounts, Usenet providers, indexers and IPTV sources each have an **Enabled** switch. A disabled source keeps its settings.
- **Friends' and Home watchlists sync, and say what they're waiting for.** Shared friends' watchlists come in through Plex's community API, and every Plex Home profile's watchlist syncs as a background job. Each title shows **Not checked yet**, **No stream found yet** or **Available**, and **Check availability** checks the whole list with live progress.
- **Your own posters** ([#445](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/445)). PosterFlow's **Asset Upload** to Plex or Jellyfin works against Ether, and you can point Ether at a poster folder. Set it up under **Integrations › PosterFlow**, then choose it as the **Poster source** under Metadata › Artwork.
- **Catalogue sources, and libraries built from them.** Everything that supplies titles is a catalogue: TMDB, plugin content kinds, and the catalogues of Stremio add-ons such as Cinemeta. They live under **Integrations › Catalogues** (one card per add-on or plugin), and **Add catalogue source** takes a Stremio add-on link or a plugin. **New library** asks where titles come from, lists every catalogue, lets you combine compatible ones, then optionally narrows them with a saved filter or a collection. A Stremio catalogue is loaded the first time a library using it is opened and kept fresh from then on.
- **Library settings make sense.** Each library's Settings page holds only that library's settings (sources, saved filter or collection, name, sharing, quality profile, which apps show it) with one Save bar, and a saved filter or collection can now be changed after the library is made. Settings that apply to all of TMDB (language, region, refresh, what's browsable, upcoming titles, new-release timing) moved to **Integrations › TMDB**, and plugin sync and setup moved to the plugin's own page.
- **Ether and Plex Media Server on one computer.** Ether never takes port 32400 when Plex Media Server is installed on the same machine. Its own port is now a setting under Apps › Plex, and remote-access checks name port conflicts in plain words.

## Playback

- **Plex starts on the first press** ([#463](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/463)). Plex apps no longer give up after 12 seconds while Ether is still finding a source; the first Play waits for the source like Jellyfin does, and stops only if the app goes away.
- **Resume is faster.** A cached resume reuses the saved probe instead of reopening the source. When you stop or pause, Ether warms the new resume point straight away. If warming finds that the remembered release is broken at that point, Ether picks, checks and warms a replacement before you press play.
- **Resume continues on the same release.** Pausing or leaving a title no longer counts its release as failed: a recovery that was about to start when you paused is cancelled, and pressing Play again starts fresh instead of skipping the release you were watching as "already tried". Releases that really failed are still avoided.
- **A slow or broken source recovers.** When a source can't keep up, Ether prepares up to three lighter copies at once. Releases that turned out incomplete are remembered and skipped. Usenet releases whose health isn't fully verified are served as a remux (same quality), so a mid-film failure can be spliced over, and health checks now sample every RAR volume.
- **Samsung TVs fall back to software encoding** when the hardware encoder fails or stalls before the first DASH segment. 10-bit HEVC and AC3 copies use the codec strings the TV expects.
- **Intel QuickSync segments are cut on real keyframes** ([#458](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/458)). QSV encodes now force IDR frames at segment boundaries, so LG and other HLS/DASH clients no longer stall at the same point. VideoToolbox uses closed GOPs.
- **Plex for iOS keeps playing when timelines go quiet** ([#456](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/456)). A session stays alive while the client is still fetching segments. Pre-roll timelines no longer return 404, and a pre-roll prepares the feature that follows it.
- **Plex Android subtitles** ([#191](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/191)). Embedded text subtitles are muxed into the stream as PMS does, and they stay when you change the player's quality.
- **Automatic subtitles work for Plex** ([#459](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/459)). With *automatic preferred* OpenSubtitles enabled, your rule's subtitle is downloaded once at playback start and selected. A manual pick still wins.
- **The right show for shared names.** A release that only matches the name loses to one whose IDs, year or episode title match. A release whose real runtime doesn't fit the episode is rejected and replaced, including a remembered one.
- **Episode titles that are initialisms match** ([#460](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/460), thanks @A54DH). "Q.R.S.T." and `Q.R.S.T.` in a release name are now the same title.
- **Jellyfin and Android clients get the quality they can play.** Device profiles from Jellyfin for Android, Streamyfin and similar apps are read properly, so a 4K HDR device is offered 4K HDR. A Dolby Vision 7 source keeps its HDR10 base layer on Apple clients.
- **Availability check timeout** ([#444](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/444), thanks @javi11). Owners with slow backends can set `plex.fileCheckTimeout` in `config.json` (default `2s`).
- **Debrid replay prefers edges that actually flow** ([#455](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/455), thanks @ogrrd).

## Apps

- **Jellyfin for Roku plays** ([#421](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/421)). Ether serves `GET /Items/{id}/Images`, which the Roku app asks for on Play. Any Jellyfin or Emby route Ether doesn't serve now answers with Jellyfin's JSON error.
- **Jellyfin Collections are back** ([#454](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/454)) and appear as a Collections library. Search returns only the item types the app asks for, so Swiftfin's Collections, Artists and Albums rows show only matches.
- **Sharp backgrounds.** Jellyfin, Emby and Plex art requests get full-resolution backdrops on large screens (they were 500 px wide). Logos load in every format the app asks for.
- **Moonfin and other Jellyfin apps** ([#405](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/405)): image tags never expire, wide cards get a landscape thumb, and list pages read only what their rows need.
- **Plex search opens the right namesake.** Ether only sends Plex the slug from a verified Plex identity, so "The Mummy (1999)" and "The Mummy (2017)" stay apart in the Plex apps.
- **Plex Web and Desktop subtitle picks work** ([#457](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/457), thanks @FarkasLabs).
- **USA TV Next and other relayed channels tune**, including compressed playlists, extensionless segments and playlists FFmpeg used to reject.

- **Filter libraries reach Plex** ([#464](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/464), thanks @machetie). Filter, collection and plugin libraries switched on for Plex appear as their own sections under their own names, a library's quality profile applies to its episodes, and Browse on a filter library no longer fails with "Filter groups are too deep".
- **Seasons show on older Plex apps** ([#468](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/468)). Plex for Android (TV) 10.x on Fire TV sent request options Ether refused, so show pages had no seasons. Ether now ignores the options PMS ignores and sorts season lists by number.
- **Jellyfin on NVIDIA Shield signs in** with the right password again.

## Plex account

- **Server token repair.** If plex.tv refuses the server's token, Ether re-exchanges it under the same server identity and retries, instead of showing "plex.tv rejects this server's sign-in". The console says exactly what failed, and the support bundle includes plex.tv's view of the server's registration (no tokens).

## Plugins

Thanks to @FizzWhirl for a precise set of issues from building a content-kind plugin.

- **`folders.hash`** ([#447](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/447)) returns size, modification time, CRC32, MD5 and SHA-1 for a file in an owner-granted folder, with cancellable background jobs and progress for large files. Digests are cached for unchanged files.
- **Local files from a kind resolver** ([#448](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/448)): `{"pattern": "file", "path": …}`, with the same checks as a Stremio `ether.file` stream.
- **Items can say they're unavailable** ([#451](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/451)). An item upserted with `unavailable: {code, detail}` stays visible with its reason and is never played.
- **Rules for plugin libraries** ([#449](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/449)), **facets on year, release date and rating** ([#450](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/450)), and **a manifest schema that covers content kinds** ([#452](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/452)).

## Owner console

- **Watch from the console.** A **Watch** button in the header opens the bundled Jellyfin web player in a new tab, already signed in as you. It uses a one-time code that expires in a minute, and the browser appears as "Ether web player" under Sign-in & devices, where you can remove it.
- Debrid and Usenet drawers follow the IPTV drawer's design, with status cards and an **Enabled** row. New connection credentials appear in a proper dialog with copy buttons and a QR code, and you can approve a TV code right on a viewer's **Sign-in & devices** page.
- **Clear cache** under the cache settings. Corrupt cached segments are dropped when they're read.
- **MDBList can be switched on again** (Save and enable failed with "no such config section").
- Logs stream live and scroll smoothly through long histories, and support bundles keep a short reason phrase for playback failures.

## Under the hood

- **Faster, safer startup.** Ether no longer walks the segment cache or backfills tables before it starts listening, and each startup phase is logged with its duration.
- **Prism.** Plex, Jellyfin and Emby behaviour now lives in each app's own frontend; shared services take capabilities instead of checking which app is asking.

## Fixes

- **New episodes that stream before they air** ([#351](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/351)). TMDB dates some episodes by their TV broadcast, days after they stream. For shows someone is watching or has on a watchlist, Ether looks for the next episode up to three days early and shows it as soon as a playable release is confirmed.
- **Debrid playback keeps finding faster sources** when the part you are watching is already cached.

- **Stremio add-ons: "Try again" works and health is real.** Ether checks an add-on the Stremio way, by reading its manifest, so an add-on marked **Not answering** recovers with one **Try again**.
- **Dubbed releases from a regional tracker show the dub's language.** A release named only `DUAL` or `MULTI` takes the language of the indexer it came from; set it under **Sources › Indexers › (indexer) › More options › Release language**.
- **OpenAI models can use tools**, through OpenAI's Responses API.
- **Live TV sources from an uploaded playlist can be edited** ([#467](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/issues/467), [#466](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/466), thanks @jmiller0). Save was greyed out for a source whose M3U or XMLTV file was uploaded rather than linked.
- **A marathon channel keeps its place** when you correct its start season or episode ([#470](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/470), thanks @activechay-lab).
- **Number fields can be cleared with backspace** while you type a new value ([#469](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/469), thanks @activechay-lab).
- **No more notification errors in the log** for playback updates that aren't notification events ([#465](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/pull/465), thanks @jmiller0).

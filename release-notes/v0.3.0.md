# Ether 0.3.0

Ether 0.3.0 serves **Plex, Jellyfin, Emby (Infuse, Swiftfin, Findroid), Silo, Stremio, WebDAV, DLNA, and M3U/XMLTV** from one install, with cross-app watch progress, shared subtitle preferences, personalized views, and Watch Party / SyncPlay rooms.

## Highlights

- **Every app, one server:** Enable Jellyfin (`8096`), Emby (`8097`), Silo (`8090`), Stremio, read-only WebDAV, DLNA, and M3U/XMLTV alongside Plex (`32400`) under **Server › Apps**. Viewers sign in with Quick Connect, device codes, or app passwords in `/ether/me` (Plex is optional).
- **Session broker and faster playback:** One playback broker enforces stream limits and owner Stop across all apps, starts Play on the first verified healthy source, yields debrid prefetch to mid-file range seeks, and adds Plex for Mac HTTP Matroska conversion, converting offline downloads, and a 1-segment DASH start buffer.
- **Redesigned owner console in 19 languages:** Organized into **Library**, **Streaming**, and **Server** sidebar groups, a single-level **Sources** page (*Debrid*, *Usenet*, *Indexers*, *Stats*), a 7-step setup wizard with a language step and finish animation, an **Overview Next steps** bar, and 19 translated locales alongside English.
- **Ranked quality profiles and new-release modes:** **Releases › Profiles** combines **Never play** filters, a draggable **What matters most** ranking ladder, and a **Tie-breaker** control. **Catalog › Settings › New releases** offers three modes (*When they can play*, *On digital release*, *Right away*), and content ratings use one age-level equivalence model (`PG` = `TV-PG`).
- **Live TV weekly virtual channels and DASH:** Build virtual channels in one drawer with a paintable weekly schedule grid in your timezone and play them over HLS and DASH in Plex Web (#204).
- **Subtitles and Plex protocol improvements:** On-pick subtitle timing alignment, mojibake repair, Plex Web subtitle upload, full browse filter negation and operators, indexed `guid=` lookups, 17-hub search with people hubs, the Plex Web server Dashboard (`/statistics/*`), `plex.tv` viewer defaults, CGNAT publish filtering, and an optional **Use Plex's metadata for Plex apps** toggle.
- **Large-library performance and guided recovery:** SQLite FTS5 trigram and people indexes, PostgreSQL `pg_trgm`, narrow browse-count indexes, zero-copy settings reads (#203, #206), a 540k-title CI performance gate, and 1-click backup restore with automatic rollback and in-app restart (#193, #202).
- **Fixes:** #141, #180, #184, #188, #189, #190, #192, #193, #196, #197, #198, #199, #200, #201, #202, #203, #204, #205, #206, #207, #208, #209, #210.

## Upgrading from 0.2.x

First start on 0.3.0 takes an automatic pre-migration snapshot and migrates your database (which can take a few minutes on large libraries). Older 0.2.x binaries cannot open a migrated 0.3.0 database. Read [**UPGRADING.md**](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/ether/blob/main/UPGRADING.md) before upgrading, especially for console navigation changes, setting up Jellyfin/Infuse/Swiftfin/Emby/Silo sign-in, and PostgreSQL HA cluster upgrades.

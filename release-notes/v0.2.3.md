Ether 0.2.3 redesigns the Overview as a compact server cockpit.

### New Overview

- **One-screen desktop layout:** Now playing and Activity sit beside a full-height Server panel, with Health and Best rows on the right. At wide, tall enough window sizes the page stays on one screen; smaller windows stack and scroll.
- **Live server traces:** CPU, memory, network and available GPU readings show recent history. Hover or touch a trace to inspect its values.
- **Health that leads with problems:** issues appear first and link to the relevant settings. Now playing and Health scroll within their panels when needed.
- **Activity at a glance:** plays, hours watched and busiest day sit above the activity chart. The previous rows of KPI cards are removed.

### Downloads

- **macOS 13+:** `Ether-0.2.3.dmg`, a universal Intel + Apple Silicon menu-bar app.
- **Windows x64:** `EtherSetup-0.2.3.exe`, with the server service and tray launcher. Standalone Windows ARM64 binaries are also available.
- **Linux amd64 / arm64:** DEB, RPM and standalone archives.
- **Docker:** `ghcr.io/braedonsaunders/ether:0.2.3` (amd64 / arm64).

Open http://localhost:32400/ether/ after starting Ether. Installers and archives bundle FFmpeg and FFprobe 8.1.3.

### Installation

The macOS app is **ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after the first launch attempt. The Windows installer is **not Authenticode-signed** and may trigger SmartScreen. The update manifest is separately signed with Ether’s Ed25519 release key.

Ether ships no content, providers, site definitions, accounts or third-party API keys. Supply your own TMDB key and service credentials. Plex and TMDB are independent third-party services; Ether is not affiliated with or endorsed by either.
